# Positive samples — the house reference library

Six references the owner sent, pre-reduced to 形/法/禁 so step-2 research and step-3 direction
boards start from sourced values, never adjectives. Each entry marks how its numbers were taken:
**[measured]** = read from CSS/tokens; **[distilled]** = read off images or frames. The standing
law covers every entry: form transfers, palettes and typefaces do not.
