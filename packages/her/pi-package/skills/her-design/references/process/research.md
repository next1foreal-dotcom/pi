# Research — observed values, not style words

Step 2 produces numbers and rules, never adjectives. "Clean, modern, minimal" is what people say
when they didn't look. "`#0A0A0A` text on `#FAFAF7`, one 72px display size, three grays, 1px
hairlines, 8px rhythm" is what looking produces. If a research note could caption any moodboard
on the internet, it observed nothing.

Before any new extraction, open research/positive-samples — the house library of pre-reduced,
sourced entries. An entry that covers the neighborhood saves the fetch.

## Three sources, in order

1. **What the owner sent.** Highest weight — it carries their taste. Reduce each piece per
   process/references (形/法/禁 — measured numbers, the rule it licenses, what must not be
   carried). Read the actual thing, whole, first.
2. **The project's own material.** For anything landing in an existing product, its tokens and
   components are research too — the project is the specification, and that authority starts
   here, not at step 8. Extract the real values (globals.css, tailwind config, neighboring
   screens) before admiring anyone else's.
3. **Real sites in the subject's neighborhood.** Two or three, chosen because they solve the
   same problem — not because they are famous. From each, extract observed values: type scale
   and its **count**, spacing rhythm, palette size, what carries hierarchy (size? weight?
   space?). Counts bind as hard as values — a scale with a fifth size is a different scale.

## The output format

One note per source, three lines each: the values (numbers), one law (a sentence you can design
by), one forbidden (what not to carry — usually the palette, always the brand). That's it.
Research notes longer than the eventual design spec are procrastination wearing glasses.

## When research is done

You can write 2–3 direction sentences (per process/direction-first: ground, density, mood, color
stance, one rejection each) **without inventing any value** — every number in them has a source.
That is the doneness test. Reaching it usually takes an hour, not a day.

## Never

- Research by adjective, or from titles and summaries — unread references are rumors.
- Keep researching past the doneness test — late research is how other people's details smuggle
  themselves into step 5.
- Research after the wireframe gate. If a real gap appears later, name it to the owner as a
  scope event, don't quietly go looking.
